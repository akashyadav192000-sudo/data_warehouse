# INSIGHTS OF DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## GLOBAL SALES & PROFIT ANALYSIS
1. Profit and sales dropped in 2012 due to major dropped in sales of bikes from United States followed by Australia
2. Major drop in sales recorded between March to July in 2012
3. 	Even after the $7 million sales of bikes, the sales of accessories and clothes were relatively less
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CUSTOMER DEMOGRAPHIC & DISTRIBUTION AANALYSIS
1. United States hold highest number of customers and France hold the least in 2013 (Recorded Highest Sales).
2. Although USA has higher number of orders, but the value of orders is greater in Australia.
3. Jordan Turner from France has the highest order value.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## PRODUCT PERFORMANCE
1. Bikes category performs the best, yielding higher profits, and clothing performs poorly.
2. Racing socks and the accessories associated with touring bike marked fewer sales.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## FINAL CONCLUSION:
1. Proper advertisement should be carried out for clothing
2. Mountain bikes are more in demand so their availability should be increased.
3. The number of accessories is less and hence different attachments and clothing should be introduced.
4. Increase average order value through category cross-selling and align inventory for mid-summer and Q4 seasonal demand surges.
5. The stocks of accessories associated with mountain and road bikes should be increased.
